# Mathematical art

A few playful experiments in geometry and motion. Flowers, flowing bands, and
wonky grids—rendered live in your browser.

[![Blumme: colorful geometric flowers with rounded petals](docs/images/blumme.png)](https://mls-m5.github.io/math-art/blumme.html)

**[Explore Mathematical art →](https://mls-m5.github.io/math-art/)**

Choose a piece and let it move. Press **Space** to pause or **R** to try something
new. The controls fade away when you leave it alone.
